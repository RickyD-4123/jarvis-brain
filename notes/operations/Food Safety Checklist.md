# Food Safety Checklist

Daily (opening):
- Check fridge temps, log them (dairy fridge must read ≤ 41°F).
- Sanitiser buckets at 200 ppm quat.
- Date-label all opened milk; discard after 3 days.

Daily (closing):
- Backflush espresso machine, soak portafilters, clean steam wands.
- Wipe down pastry case, discard unsold pastries or donate to the shelter.

Weekly:
- Deep-clean grinders and the ice machine.
- Check roastery floor and chaff bin for fire risk.

County health inspection last passed in May 2026 with a score of 97. Every new hire completes food handler certification within 30 days, covered in the [[Barista Training Program]] and the [[Employee Handbook]]. Equipment issues found during checks go into the [[Equipment Maintenance Log]].
