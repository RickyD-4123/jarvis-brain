# Green Coffee Sourcing

We buy green coffee from two importers: Northbound Imports (Colombia, Peru, Guatemala) and Sola Trading (Ethiopia, Kenya). Direct relationship with the Alvarez family farm in Huila, Colombia since 2023 — that lot is our flagship single origin.

Current contracts:
- Huila, Colombia (Alvarez) — 20 bags/year at $4.85/lb FOB, delivered in two shipments (March, September).
- Yirgacheffe, Ethiopia — 10 bags, spot buys.
- Swiss Water decaf, Peru — 6 bags.

Green coffee is our biggest cost line in the [[Monthly Budget]]. Prices rose 18% in 2026, which drove the price review in [[Pricing Strategy]]. We keep 10–12 weeks of green on hand; see [[Inventory Management]] for reorder points.

Every new lot is sample-roasted and cupped on Thursdays per the [[Roasting Schedule]]. Minimum cupping score to buy: 85.
