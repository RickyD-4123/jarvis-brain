# Inventory Management

We track inventory in a shared spreadsheet, counted every Friday by Priya.

Reorder points:
- Green coffee: reorder when any origin drops below 8 weeks of cover (see [[Green Coffee Sourcing]]).
- Retail bags (12 oz kraft, valve): reorder at 1,500 units; lead time 3 weeks.
- Milk and oat milk: daily delivery from Hillside Dairy; par levels 40 L dairy, 24 L oat.
- Pastries: delivered daily from Rise Bakery (one of our [[Local Partnerships]]).

Roasted coffee is held a maximum of 21 days. Anything older goes to the staff shelf or becomes cold brew concentrate. Waste was 4.2% of roasted volume in Q3, down from 6% after we tightened the [[Roasting Schedule]] around actual [[Online Store]] orders.

Holiday packaging stock for the [[Holiday Gift Box Campaign]] must be ordered by October 20.
