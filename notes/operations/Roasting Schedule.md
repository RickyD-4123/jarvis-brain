# Roasting Schedule

We roast four days a week on the 15 kg Diedrich, Monday to Thursday. Dan Reyes runs the roaster; Priya covers Thursdays when Dan is at the market.

- **Monday** — wholesale batches first (house espresso "Ember", then "Riverbank" drip blend). Target 18 batches.
- **Tuesday** — single origins for the [[Online Store]] and the [[Subscription Program]].
- **Wednesday** — decaf and any custom private-label roasts for [[Wholesale Accounts]].
- **Thursday** — top-up roasts, sample roasting for new lots from [[Green Coffee Sourcing]], and cupping at 2pm.

Rules: no roasting after 3pm (neighbour agreement about smoke). Every batch is logged with charge temp, first crack time and development ratio. Coffee rests 48 hours before it ships. When a batch drops more than 2% off target colour, flag it in the [[Equipment Maintenance Log]] — it's usually the drum thermocouple drifting.

Peak season (November–December) adds a Friday shift for the [[Holiday Gift Box Campaign]].
