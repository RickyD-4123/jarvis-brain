# Complaint Handling Process

Our approach is **Listen, Own, Fix, Follow up**.

1. **Listen:** let the customer finish. Don't argue about taste.
2. **Own:** "I'm sorry, that's not what we want for you."
3. **Fix:** remake any drink, no questions. Baristas can comp up to $15 without approval. For online orders, Priya can reship or refund up to $50.
4. **Follow up:** log every complaint in the shared form. Anything about illness or allergens goes straight to Maya, and the [[Food Safety Checklist]] is reviewed.

Online reviews: Maya replies to every review under 4 stars within 48 hours.

The log is summarised monthly into the [[Customer Feedback Summary]]. Wholesale complaints (e.g. stale coffee) are handled by Dan with the [[Wholesale Accounts]] contact directly. This process is taught in week 3 of the [[Barista Training Program]] and listed in the [[Employee Handbook]].
