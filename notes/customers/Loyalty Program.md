# Loyalty Program

Currently paper punch cards: buy 9 drinks, get the 10th free. About 1,100 cards in circulation, but no data.

**Relaunch on Oct 30, 2026** using Square Loyalty:
- 1 point per $1; 100 points = free drink; 250 points = free 12 oz bag.
- Double points for members of the [[Subscription Program]].
- Birthday drink.
- Existing punch cards convert at 10 points per punch.

Goals: 1,500 members by March 2027, and data to target the [[Email Newsletter]].

Promotion: [[Instagram Content Plan]], the [[Farmers Market Stall]] QR code, and baristas at the register (trained in the [[Barista Training Program]]). Estimated cost: 3% of café revenue in redemptions, already accounted for in the [[Pricing Strategy]].
