# Customer Personas

Built from the [[Customer Feedback Summary]], loyalty card data and 14 customer interviews in summer 2026.

1. **Morning Regular Rob** (35–55). Comes in at 7:45 every weekday for a drip coffee. Hates waiting. Price sensitive on drip but loyal. Most affected by the morning rush.
2. **Home Brewer Hannah** (25–40). Buys single origins, owns a pour-over kit, reads the [[Email Newsletter]]. Core [[Subscription Program]] customer. Cares about where the coffee comes from (see [[Green Coffee Sourcing]]).
3. **Weekend Wanderer Will** (families and tourists). Visits Saturdays, often via the [[Farmers Market Stall]]. Buys gifts.
4. **Office Manager Olivia**. Buys for a team and wants reliability and invoices. See [[Corporate Catering Clients]].

All personas valued honesty about price changes, an input to the [[Pricing Strategy]].
