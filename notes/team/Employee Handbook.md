# Employee Handbook

Summary of key policies (full PDF in the shared drive).

- **Pay:** barista starting rate $16.50/hr plus tips (pooled, paid weekly). +$1/hr on completing the [[Barista Training Program]].
- **Benefits:** free shift drink, one 12 oz bag per week, 30% off merchandise, paid sick leave (1 hr per 30 worked).
- **Time off:** request 2 weeks ahead in Homebase; blackout Dec 10–24.
- **Conduct:** treat customers like neighbours; follow the [[Complaint Handling Process]].
- **Safety:** food handler cert within 30 days; follow the [[Food Safety Checklist]].
- **Onboarding paperwork:** W-4, I-9, direct deposit (see [[Tax Calendar]]).

Scheduling rules are in the [[Staff Schedule]].
