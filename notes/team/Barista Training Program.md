# Barista Training Program

Four-week program run by Priya.

- **Week 1:** café tour, [[Food Safety Checklist]], register, drip and batch brew. Shadow shifts only.
- **Week 2:** espresso dial-in, milk steaming, the Ember espresso recipe (18 g in, 38 g out, 28–32 seconds).
- **Week 3:** latte art basics, customer service, [[Complaint Handling Process]], and the [[Loyalty Program]] at the register.
- **Week 4:** solo shifts with sign-off from Priya, and a cupping session with Dan to learn our coffees.

Every barista also learns daily machine care from the [[Equipment Maintenance Log]] routine. A shortened half-day version is offered free to [[Wholesale Accounts]]. Pay rises $1/hr on completion, per the [[Employee Handbook]].
