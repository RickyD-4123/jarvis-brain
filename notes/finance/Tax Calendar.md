# Tax Calendar

Copper Kettle Coffee Co. is an S-corp. Accountant: Laura Kim, CPA.

Key dates:
- **Monthly, 20th** — state sales tax return (café drinks and retail bags).
- **Quarterly** — federal payroll Form 941 (Apr 30, Jul 31, Oct 31, Jan 31). Payroll provider files it.
- **Jan 15** — Q4 estimated tax payment for Maya.
- **Jan 31** — W-2s for staff, 1099s for contractors (photographer, cleaner).
- **Mar 15** — S-corp return (Form 1120-S).
- **Apr 30** — annual financials to the lender (see [[Equipment Loan]]).

Big payments are reflected in the [[Cash Flow Forecast]]. Seasonal hires from the [[Hiring Plan]] need W-4s on day one; onboarding paperwork is listed in the [[Employee Handbook]].
