# Online Store

Shopify store at copperkettle.coffee. 19% of revenue.

Top sellers (Q3 2026):
1. Huila Colombia (Alvarez) — 12 oz, $21
2. Ember Espresso — 2 lb, $36
3. Riverbank Blend — 12 oz, $17
4. Holiday gift box (seasonal)

Shipping: free over $45, otherwise flat $6.50. Shipping cost is the biggest complaint in the [[Customer Feedback Summary]]. Conversion rate 2.8%; abandoned cart emails recover about 9%.

Orders placed by Monday 9am are roasted Tuesday per the [[Roasting Schedule]]. The store also runs the [[Subscription Program]] through the Recharge app. Product photography follows the [[Brand Guidelines]]. Traffic comes mostly from the [[Instagram Content Plan]] and the [[Email Newsletter]].
