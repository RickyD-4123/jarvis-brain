# Wholesale Accounts

Wholesale is 38% of revenue. Twelve active accounts; the top four:

1. **Lantern Books Café** — 40 lb/week of Ember espresso. Pays net-30, sometimes late.
2. **Greenway Co-op** — retail bags on shelf, 120 bags/week. Margin is thin at wholesale price.
3. **The Mill Restaurant** — 15 lb/week of Riverbank drip, plus a custom decaf.
4. **Summit Climbing Gym** — 10 lb/week, also hosts our pop-up.

Wholesale pricing: $11.50/lb for blends, $14/lb single origins, minimum 10 lb/week. We loan grinders to accounts ordering 25 lb+. Every new account gets a free barista training session based on our [[Barista Training Program]].

Late payments from Lantern Books are hurting the [[Cash Flow Forecast]]. Wholesale prices are under review in [[Pricing Strategy]]. Goal for 2027: add 5 accounts, prioritising offices from [[Corporate Catering Clients]].
