# Subscription Program

Launched February 2025. 214 active subscribers as of September 2026.

Plans:
- **Explorer** — one rotating single origin, 12 oz, every 2 weeks, $19.
- **House** — Riverbank blend, 2 lb monthly, $34.
- **Office** — 5 lb monthly, $78 (pairs with [[Corporate Catering Clients]]).

Monthly churn is 4.1%. The main cancellation reasons in the [[Customer Feedback Summary]] are "too much coffee" and shipping cost. We are testing a skip-a-month button on the [[Online Store]].

Subscribers get double points in the [[Loyalty Program]]. Subscriptions ship on Wednesdays, two days after the Tuesday single-origin roast in the [[Roasting Schedule]]. Target: 300 subscribers by end of 2026, driven by the [[Email Newsletter]] and the [[Holiday Gift Box Campaign]] (gift subscriptions).
