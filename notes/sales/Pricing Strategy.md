# Pricing Strategy

Green coffee costs rose 18% this year (see [[Green Coffee Sourcing]]), and gross margin on retail bags fell from 62% to 55%.

Decisions from the September pricing review:
- Retail 12 oz bags: +$1 across the board starting November 1.
- Café drinks: latte $5.25 → $5.50; drip unchanged at $3.25 to protect the morning regulars.
- Wholesale: +$0.75/lb at contract renewal, giving 30 days' notice to [[Wholesale Accounts]].
- Subscription prices locked for existing subscribers until March 2027 to limit churn in the [[Subscription Program]].

Expected impact: +$3,100/month gross profit, modelled in the [[Cash Flow Forecast]] and the [[Q3 Financial Review]]. Communicate the increase honestly in the [[Email Newsletter]] — customers in the [[Customer Personas]] research care most about transparency.
