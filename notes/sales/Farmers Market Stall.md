# Farmers Market Stall

Saturday Riverside Farmers Market, 8am–1pm, April through November. Dan and one barista run it.

Average Saturday: $1,150 in sales (drinks ~60%, bags ~40%). Best day this year: $2,040 at the Harvest Festival in September.

Setup: two-group lever machine on a generator, batch-brew urn, 60 retail bags. Card reader is the same Square account as the [[Online Store]].

The market is our best place to sign up new members to the [[Loyalty Program]] and the [[Subscription Program]] — we use a QR code on the banner. Feedback cards from the stall feed into the [[Customer Feedback Summary]].

Winter: the market moves indoors December–March with a smaller stall fee. We still need to decide whether to attend; see the [[Staff Schedule]] for coverage limits.
