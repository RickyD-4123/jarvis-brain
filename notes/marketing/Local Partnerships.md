# Local Partnerships

- **Rise Bakery** — supplies all café pastries daily; we supply their coffee. Revenue share on pastries, 35% to us.
- **Summit Climbing Gym** — one of our [[Wholesale Accounts]], hosts a monthly coffee pop-up and "climb and cup" event.
- **Riverside Library** — free cupping workshop once per quarter; good for [[Loyalty Program]] signups.
- **Hillside Dairy** — milk supplier; co-marketing on their delivery van.
- **Riverside Animal Shelter** — we donate unsold pastries and a portion of December gift box sales.

Every partner gets the co-branding one-pager from the [[Brand Guidelines]]. Rise Bakery deliveries are tracked in [[Inventory Management]]. Potential new partner: Greenleaf Coworking, who could become one of our [[Corporate Catering Clients]].
