# Brand Guidelines

**Voice:** warm, plain-spoken, a little nerdy about coffee but never snobby. We say "coffee from people we know," not "ethically sourced premium beans."

**Colours:** Copper #B87333, Charcoal #2B2B2B, Cream #F4EDE1. Accent green #5B7553 for seasonal.

**Type:** headlines in Recoleta, body in Inter.

**Logo:** copper kettle silhouette; minimum width 24 px; never on busy photos.

**Photography:** natural light, real hands and real customers, no stock images. Bags are shot on the oak counter.

These rules apply to the [[Online Store]], the [[Instagram Content Plan]], the [[Email Newsletter]] and all packaging, including the [[Holiday Gift Box Campaign]]. Partners in [[Local Partnerships]] get a co-branding one-pager.
